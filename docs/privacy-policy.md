# SafeInvoice privacy policy

Effective 11 October 2026 · App: SafeInvoice (`app.safeinvoice`) · Publisher: Gordon Cox, South Africa

SafeInvoice is an **offline invoicing app**. It does not use a SafeInvoice server, does not create an account, and does not collect, sell or share personal information with us.

## What the app stores

Business profiles, customers, products, invoices, payments, signatures, photos and spreadsheets that you enter stay **on this phone** in the app’s local database and files. We cannot see that information.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** SafeInvoice does not include analytics, advertising, crash reporters, or account sign-in. The release build does not use the Internet permission.

## What we share

**Nothing is shared with us or with advertisers.** If you tap Email, WhatsApp or another share action, the operating system opens the app you choose and you send a file you created. That is a one-off action you start. We do not receive a copy.

## Backups

Android Auto Backup is turned off for SafeInvoice so invoice books are not uploaded to Google Drive by the app. If you copy files off the device yourself, you control that copy.

## Children

SafeInvoice is a business tool. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect children’s data.

## Your choices

You can edit or delete customers, invoices and the business profile in the app. Uninstalling SafeInvoice removes the on-device books unless you exported them first.
