## Full description (4 000 characters)

```
SafeInvoice is offline invoicing for South African businesses. Create tax invoices on the phone, stamp a handwritten signature, and share a PDF by Email or WhatsApp. There is no account, no adverts and no SafeInvoice server. Your books stay on this device.

What you can do
• Business profile with ZAR, 15% VAT and your logo
• Customer and product catalogues with searchable dropdowns
• Invoices with line items, discount, VAT, previous balance and amount paid
• Outstanding balances in crimson, unpaid-order lists and pay-against-outstanding (cash, EFT, consignment, swapped), allocated oldest-first
• Drafts saved for later, payment notes and a customer proof-of-delivery signature
• PDF preview that matches the file Email and WhatsApp attach
• Excel / CSV import and export, plus on-device folders for receipts and notes
• Light and dark themes and a choice of accent palettes

Built for South Africa
• en-ZA dates and SAST timestamps
• Invoice numbers you control
• WhatsApp chat links (wa.me) using the customer’s number

Privacy
SafeInvoice does not collect or share data with us. Sharing a PDF uses the phone’s Email or WhatsApp app when you tap send. See the privacy policy in Settings.

SafeInvoice starts empty. There is no demo company and no sample invoices.
```
